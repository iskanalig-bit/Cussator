What Cussator does

01

Word Economy

Every word has a price. Precise terms and clear claims strengthen your position live; hedges like «kind of» and «basically» weaken it instantly, in full view.

02

Live Interruption

The AI opponent doesn't wait for the round to end. The moment it spots a vague claim, it interrupts and turns it against you — just like live debate.

03

Vocabulary Scars

Your most repeated filler words carry over between rounds, each with a stronger swap suggested — so the same weak phrase doesn't just cost you once.

04

AI Judge

After the round, a separate AI scores your performance on three criteria — Logic, Precision, Delivery — and gives you a breakdown, not just a number.
