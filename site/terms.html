Cussator ("the app," "we," "our") is a small, independently built web app for practicing live debate against an AI opponent. These Terms of Service explain the basic rules for using it. By using Cussator, you agree to these terms.

What Cussator is

Cussator is a text-based debate practice tool. You argue one side of a motion; an AI system plays the opposing side, responds to your arguments, and generates feedback (Logic, Precision, and Delivery scores, and similar) on your performance. Motions, the AI opponent's arguments, and scoring are generated automatically and are not reviewed by a person before you see them.

No accounts

Cussator has no user accounts, logins, or passwords. Anything the app remembers about your play — like your Vocabulary Scars or recent round history — is stored locally in your own browser, not on a server, and isn't tied to your identity. See our Privacy Policy for details.

AI-generated content

Debate motions, the AI opponent's arguments, and the scoring feedback are generated by a third-party AI model. This content is automatically generated and may be factually inaccurate, one-sided, repetitive, or simply wrong. Nothing the AI opponent says should be treated as factual, legal, medical, financial, or professional advice, or as an authoritative source for an actual debate, essay, or academic work.
